Common Questions

Everything you need to know

Here are honest, simple answers to questions patients and doctors frequently ask us.

Will the test hurt, and how is the sample collected?

Not at all. For adults and older children, a standard small blood draw (3–5 mL, just like a routine blood test) is done by our gentle phlebotomist in Peshawar. For babies, young children, or anyone nervous about needles, we also offer a gentle cheek swab that is completely painless and takes less than a minute but has chances of failure.

What happens if the test finds a genetic mutation?

Finding a mutation is not a verdict — it is empowering information. Our clinical lead, conducts a free post-test session to explain exactly what the result means. We work with your treating doctor to outline proven medical strategies: lifestyle adaptations, early preventive screenings, medication options, and guidance for your family.

Why is Comprehensive Gene Panel PKR 120,000 or less at Baseline?

Traditionally, labs in Pakistan ship samples to third-party commercial brokers abroad, adding shipping overhead, middleman markups, and currency surcharges. Because Baseline manages DNA processing, bioinformatics, and clinical interpretation directly with our in-house team in Peshawar, we pass those savings directly to patients.

What is "Long-Term Reanalysis" and why is it free?

Sometimes a DNA change is labeled as a "Variant of Uncertain Significance" (VUS) because science hasn't seen enough cases yet. Over months and years, as international medical databases update, these variants get resolved. Other labs charge you for a whole new test; Baseline keeps your data securely archived and updates your findings for free as science advances.

Is my genetic data safe and private?

Strictly confidential. Your personal name and identifying details are detached and replaced with an anonymous barcode before any computer analysis begins. Your DNA information is never sold, licensed, or shared with insurance companies or third parties.

How can my doctor collaborate with Baseline?

Doctors can call, message on WhatsApp, or refer patients directly. We provide doctors with clear, actionable clinical reports (following international ACMG/AMP guidelines) and our bioinformatics team is always available for direct case conferences. We also design custom specialty panels for clinics at no extra charge.
